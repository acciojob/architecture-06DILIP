Objective

Nature surrounds us with beauty, fresh air, and life every single day. Green trees stand tall and give us the oxygen we need to breathe. Colorful flowers bloom in the spring and bring joy to our eyes. Rivers and streams flow gently to provide water for all living things. Birds sing sweet songs from the branches of leafy bushes and trees. Animals find safe homes and delicious food inside quiet forests. The bright sun warms the earth and helps tiny seeds grow into plants. Gentle rain falls from the sky to keep the soil rich and wet. We must protect our environment by planting more trees and keeping places clean. Loving and caring for nature ensures a healthy and happy planet for everyone.
